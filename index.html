About GrexLabs

Who runs GrexLabs, and how paying works

Who runs GrexLabs?
GrexLabs is operated by GrexLabs Studio, India-first with worldwide availability. Questions go to support@grexlabs.in and are answered by email.
How do I pay, and is there a subscription?
No subscription is needed. You start with $10 in free credit when you verify your email, then top up a USD balance whenever you like — every top-up is visible in your account. Monthly plans (Pro $29, Premium $99) are optional, for people who make a lot. See pricing.
What am I charged for?
Only for tokens on non-free models. Each request records its usage in your dashboard before the balance moves, free :free models never touch your credit, and top-up credit never expires.
Can I try it before paying?
Yes. 31 model ids ending in :free plus auto:free run with zero authentication — no signup, no key, no credit card. A new account also gets $10 in starter credit for flagship models.
Which formats does the API speak?
Three, on one endpoint: OpenAI chat completions (/v1/chat/completions), Anthropic messages (/v1/messages) and Google Gemini generateContent (/v1/generateContent) — official SDKs work unchanged. Details on the API page.
Do I need an account to try it?
No. Every model id ending in :free plus auto:free runs without a key, signup or card. Paid models ask for a free account so we can bill the token usage to your $10 starter credit — signup takes under a minute.
How fast are responses?
Free routes usually stream the first token in under a second. Flagship models vary by lab and load; the router races healthy upstreams with a 12-second per-attempt budget so a slow provider never stalls your app.
Can I use this from India?
Yes — GrexLabs is India-first with worldwide edge routing, UPI-friendly top-ups in USD balance, and support at support@grexlabs.in. Latency to major labs is tuned from Indian regions.
What happens when my free credit runs out?
Free :free models keep working forever with no key. For flagships, top up in USD from $5 — credit never expires, and every request shows its price before you're billed.
Is there a cookie policy?
We use only essential cookies for sign-in and session security, plus optional analytics to improve the product. You can accept or decline non-essential cookies in the banner, and change your mind anytime via privacy.
